# Showcase site (all five pages)

Mode: Persuade (Home, Platform, Contact); Experience (Work); Read (About).
Audience: prospective clients in agency pitches, laptop or large screen. Action: see the platform, then contact.
Constraints: see PRODUCT.md and .claude/docs/REDESIGN_PLAN.md (full plan, palette, tokens, blocks).

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a swatch book of its own system: named, coded colour chips that can be swapped. It refuses the cream-plus-italic-serif studio template and the dark SaaS hero.
OWN-WORLD: Chalk #EDEFE7 ground 60%, bottle #0F3B30 fields 30%, flare #FF3D7F 10%. Sections are whole chip fields. Archivo expanded display, Geist Mono chip codes. Square corners, hairline rules, white code strips under images. One duotone image treatment.
STORY: The visitor sees a polished studio site, then sees it is made of swappable blocks and tokens, and believes their project could start here. They go to Platform or Contact.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: a fan deck of five image chips riveted bottom-left, filling the right 55%. A heading-3xl two-line headline on the left, lead text, flare primary button "See the platform" plus a secondary link. A sticky header with mono numbered nav and a flare CTA.
FORM: grounded candidate 6 of 7 (swatch / chip book). seed:37066ea21d518b57. Raises: one shared motion beat (from the VU bridge), duotone unification of imagery (from the indoor sun), a numbered margin column for the Platform flow (from the orizuru).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
